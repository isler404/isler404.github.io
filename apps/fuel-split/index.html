Fuel Split

Split gas money for road trips

Work out exactly what everyone owes for fuel, tolls and parking on a shared drive.

Features

  • Cost per person for any number of passengers
  • Round trips, tolls and parking included in the split
  • km or miles; L/100 km, km/L, MPG (US) or MPG (UK)
  • Share the result to your group chat in one tap
  • Works offline

Fuel Split Pro

  • Garage: save all your vehicles and pick one with a tap
  • Trip history with every cost and split
  • No ads

Frequently asked questions

Which units can I use?

Distance in kilometres or miles. Consumption in L/100 km, km/L, US MPG or UK MPG. The fuel price is per litre for metric units and per gallon for MPG.

Does Fuel Split need an internet connection?

No. All calculations happen on your iPhone. Only ads need a connection.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Fuel Split and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
