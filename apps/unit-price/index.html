Frequently asked questions

Can I compare different units?

Yes, as long as they measure the same thing: weight with weight, volume with volume, or items with items. Grams, kilograms, ounces and pounds can be mixed freely.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is calculated on your iPhone; only ads need a connection.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Unit Price Check and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
