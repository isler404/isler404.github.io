Hex Sweeper Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-29 · Developer: Enescan İşler

Hex Sweeper is built to keep your information on your device. There is no account, and the developer does not run a server that receives your data.

Information that stays on your device

  • Your best time for each difficulty

This information is stored only in the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Device permissions

  • None.

You can change permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first and can change their choice at any time from the app's settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Subscribers to Hex Sweeper Pro do not see ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details. Apple shares only whether a subscription is active.

Children

Hex Sweeper is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Ad tracking: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland): Settings tab in the app → Privacy choices.
  • Remove all app data: delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
