Frequently asked questions

How is it different from classic minesweeper?

Every cell is a hexagon with six neighbours instead of eight, so numbers top out at 6 and patterns read differently.

Is the first tap safe?

Yes. Mines are placed after your first tap, never on it or next to it.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Hex Sweeper and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
