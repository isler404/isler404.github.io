Frequently asked questions

Is every puzzle solvable?

Yes. Every board is created by pressing lamps on a dark board, so there's always a way back.

How do hints work?

With Pro, a hint marks one lamp that is part of a shortest solution for the board as it is now.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Lights Out Deco and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
