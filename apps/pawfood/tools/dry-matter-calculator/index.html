Dry matter calculator

Enter the guaranteed analysis from the bag or can. The calculator removes the water so you can compare wet and dry foods on the same basis. It runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Dry matter basis

How the calculation works

Dry matter is what is left when all water is removed. Dry matter % = 100 − moisture %.

Dry matter value = as-fed value ÷ dry matter % × 100. Example: a wet food with 10% protein and 78% moisture has 22% dry matter, so protein on a dry matter basis is 10 ÷ 22 × 100 ≈ 45%.

Carbohydrates are rarely listed on pet food labels. They are estimated by difference (nitrogen-free extract): 100 − protein − fat − fiber − moisture − ash. This needs all five values. Many labels do not state ash; the calculator then shows no estimate instead of assuming a number.

Guaranteed analysis values are minimums (protein, fat) and maximums (fiber, moisture, ash), not exact amounts, so every result is an estimate.

Estimate only — check the feeding guide on the bag and ask your veterinarian.

FAQ

Why compare on dry matter?

Wet food is mostly water. A can with 9% protein and a kibble with 27% protein can carry a similar share of protein once the water is removed. Dry matter puts both on the same footing.

Why doesn't the calculator assume a typical ash value?

Ash differs a lot between recipes, roughly from about 5% to over 10% of dry matter. Assuming a value would make the carbohydrate estimate look more precise than it is.

Is a higher protein percentage better?

Not by itself. Needs depend on species, life stage and health. Use the numbers to compare foods, and ask your veterinarian what suits your pet.
