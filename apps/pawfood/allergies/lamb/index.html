Lamb and mutton allergy in dogs and cats: what to check on the label

Last updated 7 November 2026 · By Pawfood

If your vet has confirmed a lamb allergy in your dog or cat, or you simply avoid lamb, the ingredients list is where you check. This page lists the label names that always mean lamb, the vaguer terms that might, and how Pawfood flags both.

What counts as lamb

Lamb in Pawfood means sheep of any age: lamb, mutton and the meals, organs, tripe, fat and broth made from them. Sheep's milk is in the dairy family, not here. Lamb was once common in diets sold as an alternative to beef and chicken.

Terms that might mean lamb

These wordings can contain lamb, but the label doesn't say exactly what they come from: natural flavor, meat, meat and animal derivatives, meat meal, meat by-products, meat by-product meal, animal protein, animal fat, tallow, animal digest, liver.

Meat, meat meal, meat by-products, tallow and animal fat may come from sheep or other animals, and natural flavor and liver don't name a species.

Fats, oils and hydrolyzed forms: less protein, not none

Food allergies are reactions to proteins (Merck Veterinary Manual), and processing changes how much intact protein an ingredient carries. Pawfood rates these as reduced carryover: lamb fat, hydrolyzed lamb protein, natural lamb flavor, mutton fat. Reduced is not zero. Fats and oils can hold traces of protein, and hydrolysis breaks proteins into smaller pieces without removing every reactive fragment. Whether your pet can have these forms is a question for your vet.

How often it's reported in dogs and cats

Mueller, Olivry and Prélaud (2016) pooled published studies of dogs and cats with food allergy. The reported allergen sources included lamb in about 5% of dogs. For cats, lamb is among the less often reported sources. Many animals react to more than one food, so the figures overlap. They describe those studies, not your own pet's odds.

Label checklist for lamb

  • Read the full ingredients list, not the front of the pack. Under AAFCO naming rules a "beef dinner" needs only 25% beef, and "with" or "flavor" names need far less.
  • Check every name in the table above, including fats, broths, digests and plasma.
  • Treat unnamed wordings ("meat", "animal fat", "natural flavor") as unknown and ask the manufacturer what they are made from.
  • Read inside parentheses and the vitamin and mineral part of the list.
  • "Mutton" and "sheep" mean the same family as lamb.
  • Lamb-led recipes often also use chicken fat or fish oil; check those if they are on your list too.
  • Recheck each new bag and each product separately: recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed.

Treats, toppers and chews

Lamb ears, lamb lung bites and lamb jerky are common treat sources. Check treats, chews, toppers and flavored supplements like food, and ask your vet before giving any during an elimination diet.

How Pawfood flags lamb

When lamb is on a pet's avoid list, Pawfood compares each ingredient it recognizes with the lists above:

  • A listed lamb ingredient: Match found if you saved lamb as a vet-confirmed allergy or as something you just avoid; Worth a closer look if you saved it as a suspected sensitivity.
  • A reduced-carryover form (fat, oil, hydrolyzed, flavor): still a match for a vet-confirmed allergy, with a note that it carries less protein.
  • A term that might mean lamb: Worth a closer look, because the label doesn't name the source.
  • No recorded match: nothing on the listed ingredients matched your records. It doesn't mean the food is allergen-free; labels can be incomplete and recipes change.

Words Pawfood doesn't recognize are shown as unrecognized, so check those against the label photo yourself.

Talk to your vet

Pawfood explains labels; it doesn't diagnose, treat or prescribe diets. Food allergy is usually confirmed with an elimination diet and food challenge run by a vet, so talk to yours before removing lamb or switching foods. If your pet has trouble breathing, facial swelling, repeated vomiting or collapses, contact a vet or emergency clinic right away.
