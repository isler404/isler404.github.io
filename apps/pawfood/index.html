Pawfood

Pet food ingredient scanner for dogs and cats

Scan a pet food label or barcode and see how its ingredients line up with your pet's allergies, sensitivities and the things you'd rather avoid. Compare foods on a dry-matter basis and keep a simple food, symptom and weight journal.

Pawfood keeps your pets' data on your device. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Support
Questions or a problem with a result? Email support@opsisdigital.com.

Pawfood is not a veterinary service and doesn't diagnose or treat. "No recorded match" never means a food is allergen-free. Ask your veterinarian about your pet's diet.
