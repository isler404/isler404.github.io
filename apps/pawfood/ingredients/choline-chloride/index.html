Choline chloride

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: A source of choline, an essential nutrient.

What it is

On the label it is a vitamin source.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place choline chloride in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

Pawfood has no species-specific rule for choline chloride. For questions about your own pet's diet, your veterinarian is the right person to ask.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add choline chloride itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
