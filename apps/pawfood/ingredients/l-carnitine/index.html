L-carnitine

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: A nutrient involved in fat metabolism, added as a supplement.

What it is

On the label it is a supplement.

L-carnitine helps the body use fat for energy. It is often added to weight-management foods. Dogs and cats normally make it themselves.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place L-carnitine in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

Pawfood has no species-specific rule for L-carnitine. For questions about your own pet's diet, your veterinarian is the right person to ask.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add L-carnitine itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
