Potassium sorbate

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: A mould inhibitor used in moist foods and treats.

What it is

On the label it is a preservative or antioxidant.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place potassium sorbate in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

Pawfood has no species-specific rule for potassium sorbate. For questions about your own pet's diet, your veterinarian is the right person to ask.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add potassium sorbate itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
