Titanium dioxide

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: A white mineral pigment used as a colour.

What it is

On the label it is a color additive.

Titanium dioxide is a white pigment used to brighten kibble pieces or treats. It has no nutritional role.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place titanium dioxide in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

Pawfood has no species-specific rule for titanium dioxide. For questions about your own pet's diet, your veterinarian is the right person to ask.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add titanium dioxide itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
