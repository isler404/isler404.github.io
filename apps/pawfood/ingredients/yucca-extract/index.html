Yucca schidigera extract

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: Extract of the Yucca schidigera plant, used to reduce stool odour.

What it is

Extracts are concentrated components pulled from a plant or other material with water, alcohol or oil. They are used in small amounts for a specific purpose, such as preservation or odor control.

On the label it is a supplement.

Yucca schidigera extract is added in small amounts to reduce stool odor.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place yucca schidigera extract in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

Pawfood has no species-specific rule for yucca schidigera extract. For questions about your own pet's diet, your veterinarian is the right person to ask.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add yucca schidigera extract itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
