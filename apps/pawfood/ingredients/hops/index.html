Hops

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: Hop flowers, used in brewing.

What it is

Dried, dehydrated or freeze-dried forms have the water removed without rendering. Like a meal, they are concentrated, so a small share of the recipe can still carry a lot of protein.

On the label it is an ingredient with another function.

Hops are listed as toxic to dogs by the ASPCA. They are a risk mainly around home brewing, not in commercial pet food.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place hops in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

For dogs, Pawfood shows this note: “Hops are listed as toxic to dogs.” (the result becomes Match found).

Questions about your pet's health? Ask your vet.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add hops itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
