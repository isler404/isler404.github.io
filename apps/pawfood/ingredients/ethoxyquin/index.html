Ethoxyquin

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: A synthetic antioxidant preservative, also used in fish meal.

What it is

On the label it is a preservative or antioxidant.

Ethoxyquin is a synthetic antioxidant. In the United States it is permitted in animal feed within a limit. In the EU its authorisation as a feed additive was denied by Regulation (EU) 2022/1375. It can reach a food through an ingredient such as fish meal that was stabilized before delivery, in which case it may not appear on the final label.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place ethoxyquin in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

For dogs and cats, Pawfood shows this note: “Ethoxyquin is a synthetic preservative permitted in some markets; it is not authorised as a feed additive in the EU (Regulation (EU) 2022/1375), and its use is debated.” (information only, the result does not change).

Questions about your pet's health? Ask your vet.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add ethoxyquin itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
