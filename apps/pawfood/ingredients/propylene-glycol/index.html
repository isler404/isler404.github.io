Propylene glycol

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: A humectant used in some semi-moist dog foods and treats.

What it is

On the label it is an ingredient with another function.

Propylene glycol keeps semi-moist foods and treats soft. In the United States it is not permitted in or on cat food (21 CFR 589.1001), because cats are sensitive to it. It may still appear in dog treats, so households with both species should check treats too.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place propylene glycol in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

For cats, Pawfood shows this note: “Propylene glycol is not permitted in cat food in the United States (21 CFR 589.1001).” (the result becomes Match found).

Questions about your pet's health? Ask your vet.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add propylene glycol itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
