Carrageenan

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: A thickener extracted from red seaweed, common in wet foods.

What it is

Extracts are concentrated components pulled from a plant or other material with water, alcohol or oil. They are used in small amounts for a specific purpose, such as preservation or odor control.

On the label it is a thickener, gelling agent or binder.

Carrageenan is a seaweed extract that thickens and sets wet food and gravies. It is permitted in pet food. Its effect on the gut is debated, and some owners prefer to avoid it; it is not an allergen family in Pawfood, but you can add it to a pet's list as an ingredient to avoid.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place carrageenan in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

Pawfood has no species-specific rule for carrageenan. For questions about your own pet's diet, your veterinarian is the right person to ask.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add carrageenan itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
