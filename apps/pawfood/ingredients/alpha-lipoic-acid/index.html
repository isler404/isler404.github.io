Alpha-lipoic acid

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: An antioxidant used in some supplements and foods.

What it is

On the label it is a supplement.

Alpha-lipoic acid is an antioxidant supplement. Research found cats tolerate it at much lower doses than dogs, and the amount in a food is usually not stated, so Pawfood marks it as worth a closer look for cats.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place alpha-lipoic acid in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

For cats, Pawfood shows this note: “Cats tolerate alpha-lipoic acid at much lower doses than dogs; the amount in the food is usually not stated.” (the result becomes at least Worth a closer look).

Questions about your pet's health? Ask your vet.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add alpha-lipoic acid itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
