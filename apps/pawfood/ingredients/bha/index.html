BHA

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: Butylated hydroxyanisole, a synthetic antioxidant preservative.

What it is

On the label it is a preservative or antioxidant.

BHA (butylated hydroxyanisole) is a synthetic antioxidant that keeps fats from going rancid. It is permitted within limits in the US and the EU. Some owners prefer foods preserved with tocopherols instead; Pawfood shows an informational note rather than a warning.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place BHA in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

For dogs and cats, Pawfood shows this note: “This synthetic antioxidant preservative is permitted within limits; some owners prefer to avoid it.” (information only, the result does not change).

Questions about your pet's health? Ask your vet.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add BHA itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
