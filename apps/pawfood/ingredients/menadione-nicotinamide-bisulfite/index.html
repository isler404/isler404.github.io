Menadione nicotinamide bisulfite

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: A synthetic source of vitamin K activity.

What it is

On the label it is a vitamin source.

This is a synthetic source of vitamin K (vitamin K3) that is permitted in pet food. Some owners prefer foods without it; it is used at low levels to supply vitamin K activity.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place menadione nicotinamide bisulfite in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

Pawfood has no species-specific rule for menadione nicotinamide bisulfite. For questions about your own pet's diet, your veterinarian is the right person to ask.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add menadione nicotinamide bisulfite itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
