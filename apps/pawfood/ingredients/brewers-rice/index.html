Brewers rice

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: Broken rice fragments left from rice milling.

What it is

"By-products" are the parts of the animal other than muscle meat, such as organs, blood and other clean tissue. They are a regulated feed term, not a quality grade, and they carry the protein of the species they come from.

On the label it is a carbohydrate (energy) source.

Brewers rice is the small fragments of rice kernels separated from whole kernels during milling. It is a digestible carbohydrate source. The name refers to the size of the pieces, which brewers also use, not to beer.

Allergen family

Pawfood counts brewers rice in the Rice and Cereal grains (any) family.

Rice is among the food allergen sources most often reported in dogs (Mueller, Olivry and Prélaud 2016).

Protein carryover

Protein carryover describes how much of the source's protein an ingredient is expected to carry into the food. Food allergies in dogs and cats are reactions to proteins, so the same species can matter more in one form than another.

High. This ingredient is expected to carry the protein of its source in full. If the source is on your pet's list, it matters as much as the meat itself.

Dogs and cats

Pawfood has no species-specific rule for brewers rice. For questions about your own pet's diet, your veterinarian is the right person to ask.

How Pawfood flags it

If a pet's list has Rice and Cereal grains (any) saved as a vet-confirmed allergy or as something you avoid, brewers rice gives Match found. Saved as a suspected sensitivity, it gives Worth a closer look.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
