Sodium benzoate

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: Benzoic acid or its salt, a preservative to which cats are sensitive.

What it is

On the label it is a preservative or antioxidant.

Sodium benzoate is a preservative. Cats are more sensitive to benzoic acid and benzoates than many other species, so Pawfood shows an informational note for cats.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place sodium benzoate in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

For cats, Pawfood shows this note: “Cats are more sensitive to benzoic acid and benzoates than many other species.” (information only, the result does not change).

Questions about your pet's health? Ask your vet.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add sodium benzoate itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
