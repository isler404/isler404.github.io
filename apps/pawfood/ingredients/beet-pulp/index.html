Beet pulp

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: Fiber left after sugar extraction from sugar beets.

What it is

Fiber ingredients are the structural parts of plants. They add bulk and affect stool quality and how filling a food is. They carry little or no protein.

On the label it is a fiber source.

Beet pulp is the fibrous material left after sugar is extracted from sugar beets. Most of the sugar is gone; it is used as a moderately fermentable fiber.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place beet pulp in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

Pawfood has no species-specific rule for beet pulp. For questions about your own pet's diet, your veterinarian is the right person to ask.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add beet pulp itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
