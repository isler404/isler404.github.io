Guar gum

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In short: A thickener from guar beans.

What it is

Extracts are concentrated components pulled from a plant or other material with water, alcohol or oil. They are used in small amounts for a specific purpose, such as preservation or odor control.

On the label it is a thickener, gelling agent or binder.

Guar gum is a plant-based thickener from guar beans, a legume. It is used in small amounts in wet food.

Allergen family

Pawfood does not place guar gum in an allergen family. You can still add it to a pet's list as an individual ingredient to avoid.

Dogs and cats

Pawfood has no species-specific rule for guar gum. For questions about your own pet's diet, your veterinarian is the right person to ask.

How Pawfood flags it

It is flagged only if you add guar gum itself to a pet's list.

This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free. Labels can be incomplete, recipes change, and shared equipment isn't listed. If your pet has a diagnosed allergy, check with your vet.
