If my dog is allergic to chicken, can it have turkey or duck?

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

Maybe not. Birds share similar proteins, and lab studies found most chicken-sensitized dogs also recognized turkey and duck. Ask your vet before trying.

On this page

  2. What the evidence shows
  3. Watch for "poultry"

It isn't certain. Chicken, turkey and duck are related birds with similar muscle proteins. In a lab study, 97% of blood samples from dogs with chicken-specific antibodies also recognized duck and turkey meat (Olivry, Bexley and Mougeot 2017). Antibody recognition in a test tube doesn't prove a dog will react when eating the food, so whether turkey or duck fits your dog's diet is a question for your vet.

Why related species matter

The immune system reacts to the shape of specific proteins. Closely related animals make similar versions of many proteins, so antibodies against one can bind another. This is called cross-reactivity. It is why vets planning an elimination diet often avoid all birds for a chicken-allergic dog rather than switching to another poultry.

What the evidence shows

  • Dogs: blood from 40 dogs with chicken-specific IgE recognized turkey and duck in 97% of cases (Olivry, Bexley and Mougeot 2017).
  • Cats: in the same study, 84% of cat samples recognized duck and 97% turkey.
  • Fish: in another lab study, some dogs with chicken antibodies also had cross-reactive antibodies to fish (Bexley, Kingswell and Olivry 2019). Whether that matters clinically isn't known.

These studies looked at antibodies, not reactions in everyday feeding. Some chicken-allergic dogs do eat turkey or duck without problems. Only a supervised food challenge tells you about your own dog.

Watch for "poultry"

"Poultry", "poultry meal", "poultry fat" and "natural poultry flavor" can contain chicken, turkey, duck or other birds. A "duck" recipe can also list chicken fat or chicken broth further down. Read the whole list.

Talk to your vet

If your vet has confirmed a chicken allergy, ask whether to avoid all poultry, and whether a novel protein or a hydrolysed diet makes more sense. A food challenge with turkey or duck can be planned under supervision if you want to know.
