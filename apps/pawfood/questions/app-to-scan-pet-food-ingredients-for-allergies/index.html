Is there an app to scan dog food ingredients for allergies?

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

Yes. Pawfood is an iPhone app that scans dog and cat food labels and checks each ingredient against your pet's allergens and avoid list.

Yes. Pawfood is an iPhone and iPad app that scans a dog or cat food label (barcode or ingredient list) and checks every ingredient against the list you keep for each pet: allergies your vet confirmed, sensitivities you suspect, and anything you simply want to avoid. It explains labels; it doesn't diagnose or tell you a food is right for your pet. Other label and pet nutrition apps exist too, so pick the one that fits how you work.

What Pawfood does

  • Scans a label. Scan a barcode, photograph the ingredient list or pick a photo. Text is read on the device, and you can correct any word the camera misread.
  • Checks each pet. Each scan is checked against all your pets at once, each with its own species, life stage and avoid list.
  • Recognizes other names. "Chicken meal", "Hühnermehl" and "tavuk unu" are matched to the same family. The knowledge base covers 767 ingredients and 76 allergen families, in six app languages plus common Italian and Dutch names.
  • Flags vague terms. "Poultry fat", "animal digest" or "natural flavor" don't name a species, so they're shown as worth a closer look instead of being guessed.

The four results

  1. Match found: an ingredient belongs to something on your pet's list.
  2. Worth a closer look: something might match, such as a vague term or a suspected sensitivity.
  3. No recorded match: nothing on the listed ingredients matched your records. This doesn't mean the food is allergen-free; labels can be incomplete and shared equipment isn't listed.
  4. Not enough data: too little of the label could be read, so there is no result.

Free and Pawfood Plus

The free version includes 3 scans with full result status and allergen matches. Pawfood Plus, a subscription, adds unlimited scans, the full ingredient breakdown, dry matter and carbohydrate estimates, side-by-side comparison of up to four foods, a food journal and a PDF summary for your vet.

Privacy

Pets, lists, scans and journal entries stay on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account. Scanning and matching work offline; optional online lookups send only a barcode number or a brand name.

What no app can do

No scanner can see cross-contact from shared equipment, recipe changes that aren't on the label yet, or how your pet will react. Use the result as a starting point and talk to your vet about food allergies.
