What is animal digest in pet food?

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

Animal digest is animal tissue broken down by enzymes or heat into a liquid or powder flavor coating. The species is often not stated.

Animal digest is animal tissue that has been broken down with enzymes, heat or both into a liquid or powder. It is mainly used as a palatability enhancer, often sprayed onto kibble to make it more appealing. When the label says only "animal digest", the species isn't named, so it could come from poultry, beef, pork, fish or other animals.

How it's made

Clean animal tissue, not including hair, horns, teeth or hooves, is broken down by controlled enzyme activity or hydrolysis. The result is concentrated in flavor. It is a legitimate, defined feed ingredient; using it doesn't mean the food is low quality.

Named vs unnamed digest

  • "Chicken digest" or "fish digest" names the main source.
  • "Poultry digest" narrows it to birds, but not which ones.
  • "Animal digest" or "liver digest" doesn't name the species at all.

For most pets this makes no difference. For a pet with a confirmed food allergy, or during an elimination diet, an unnamed digest is an unknown.

Does digestion remove the allergen?

Not reliably. Digests are partly broken-down protein, and the degree of breakdown isn't controlled to allergy standards or stated on the label. A study of hydrolysed poultry proteins found that only extensive hydrolysis prevented antibody recognition in dogs and cats with chicken-specific antibodies (Olivry, Bexley and Mougeot 2017). Treat a digest as carrying the protein of its source unless your vet says otherwise.

Where it appears

Animal digest is common in dry dog and cat food and in treats, usually after the main ingredients. EU labels may include it within "meat and animal derivatives", which also doesn't name the species unless the label adds it.

What you can do

If your pet has an avoid list, ask the manufacturer which species the digest comes from, and whether that can change between batches. Keep the answer with the date.
