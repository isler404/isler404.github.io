What are the most common cat food allergens?

Last updated 12 November 2026 · By Pawfood

In published studies, beef, fish and chicken were the most often reported food allergen sources in cats, followed by wheat. Not a risk ranking for your cat.

In a review of published studies of cats with food allergies, the most often reported sources were beef (about 18% of cats), fish (about 17%) and chicken (about 5%), with wheat close behind (Mueller, Olivry and Prélaud 2016). Many cats reacted to more than one food. These figures describe which foods were reported in studies, not how likely your own cat is to react.

Source: Mueller, Olivry and Prélaud 2016. Dairy, corn and others were reported less often. The cat data come from far fewer animals than the dog data, so the percentages are less precise.

Why these foods?

Beef, fish and chicken have long been common in cat food. The more often a protein is eaten, the more chances there are for a cat to become sensitized, and the more often it shows up in studies.

What food allergy looks like in cats

In a review of 210 cats, most were itchy, especially on the head, face and neck, and many had hair loss from overgrooming or scabby skin (Olivry and Mueller 2019). Vomiting and diarrhea can also occur. Other conditions look the same, so only your vet can work out what's going on, usually with an elimination diet and a food challenge.

Where these proteins hide

Fish often appears as fish oil, fish broth or "ocean fish"; chicken as chicken fat, broth or digest; beef inside "meat" or "meat and animal derivatives". "Animal digest" and "natural flavor" don't name their source at all. The cat allergen guide lists where to look.
