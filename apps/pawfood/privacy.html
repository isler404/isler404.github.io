Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Pawfood is made by Opsis Digital. The short version: we don't collect your pet data or anything that identifies you. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. The only thing the app sends to a service we use is an anonymous record of subscription purchases (see Purchases below).

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Pet profiles: name, species, age, weight, breed, photo, ingredients to flag, vet diet notes.
  • Scans and their results, favorites, comparisons.
  • Journal entries, weights, food periods, stock and reminder settings.
  • App preferences such as units and country.

We can't see this data and it is never sent to us. Device backups you make (for example through iCloud or your computer) may include this app's data; those backups are managed by Apple and under your control.

Camera and photos

The camera and photos you pick are used only to read barcodes and label text on your device. Photos are processed on device and are not uploaded.

Optional online lookups

If "Online lookups" is on (you can turn it off in Settings), the app may send:

  • A product barcode number to Open Pet Food Facts, to find product information.
  • A brand name to openFDA (U.S. Food and Drug Administration), to check public recall records.

These requests contain no account, pet or personal information. Like any internet request, the services can see your IP address; their own privacy policies apply. If you turn the setting off, Pawfood uses only the data built into the app.

Contributions

If you choose to send a product correction or report, the app opens it for you to review before anything is sent, and shows where it goes. Nothing is sent automatically.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We receive no payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device, and that check alone decides which features you can use.

When you open the subscription screen, buy or restore a subscription, the app sends an anonymous purchase record to RevenueCat, the subscription-analytics service we use, so we can see how subscriptions are doing (for example renewals and cancellations). The record contains the product, price and currency, purchase and renewal dates, App Store transaction identifiers and your App Store country, plus basic technical details such as app version and system version. It is linked to a random identifier created by the app, not to your name, email address, Apple Account, pets or anything you entered in the app. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is not combined with data from other apps or websites. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). Like any internet request, the service can see your IP address. If you never open the subscription screen, nothing is sent to RevenueCat.

Notifications

Reminders (for example, food running low) are scheduled locally on your device. We don't send push marketing.

Children

Pawfood is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Export and deletion

You can export all your data as a file and delete all data from Settings → Data. Deleting the app also removes its data from the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
