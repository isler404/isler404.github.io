E120: Cochineal, carminic acid, carmines

Food additive · Colour

What it does

  • Colour: Adds or restores colour.

Good to know

Made from insects. Not vegan; many vegetarians avoid it too.

Other names on labels

Cochineal, Carmine, Carminic Acid, CI 75470, Natural Red 4, Cochinilla, Cochonilha, Karmin, Cochenille
