E222: Sodium hydrogen sulphite

Food additive · Preservative, Antioxidant

What it does

  • Preservative: Protects against spoilage by microbes.
  • Antioxidant: Slows oxidation, such as fats going rancid.

Good to know

A sulphite. Above 10 mg/kg, sulphites must be declared as an allergen.

Other names on labels

Sulphur Dioxide, Calcium Sulphite, Natrii Sulfis, Potassium Bisulphite, Potassium Metabisulphite, Sodium Bisulphite, Sodium Metabisulfite, Sodium Metabisulphite, Sodium Sulfite
