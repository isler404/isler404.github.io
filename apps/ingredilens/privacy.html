IngrediLens Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-17 · Developer: Enescan İşler

IngrediLens reads ingredient lists on product labels and compares them with filters you choose. It is built so that what you tell it about your health and diet stays on your iPhone. This policy explains what stays on your device, what leaves it, and why.

Health and diet filters: never leave your device

The allergens, skin conditions, dietary preferences and custom terms you select, your scan history and the label text the app reads are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are not uploaded, not sent to the developer, not shared with advertisers or any other third party, and never used for advertising, ad targeting or measurement. You can export or delete them at any time from the Profile tab. Deleting the app removes them.

Camera and photos

The camera and photos you choose are processed on your device with Apple's on-device text recognition. Images are not saved by the app and are not uploaded.

Barcode lookup (optional, off by default)

If you turn on "Look up barcodes online", only the barcode digits are sent to Open Food Facts or Open Beauty Facts to fetch a product's ingredient list. Nothing about you or your filters is sent. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

Sign in with Apple is used only to keep Premium access across your devices. The developer receives an anonymous Apple user identifier and does not receive your health filters or scan history.

Advertising in the free version

The free version shows banner ads and occasional full-screen ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are never shown on top of a result. To show, limit and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, and how you interact with ads;
  • diagnostic data about ad performance.

No ad request contains your filters, scan history, label text or anything you entered. When the app asks whether it may track you (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), you can decline; the app works the same either way and you will see non-personalised ads. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first and can change their choice at any time under Profile → Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

IngrediLens Premium subscribers see no ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

Children

IngrediLens is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Ad tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland): Profile → Ad privacy choices.
  • Remove ads: IngrediLens Premium.
  • Delete your filters and history: Profile → Delete all my data, or delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
